Kids Toon Hub Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

The short version

Kids Toon Hub is a cartoon app for children. We do not ask for, collect, or keep any personal information about your child or you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools in the app.

Information we collect

None. The app never asks for a name, email address, phone number, photo, voice recording, contacts or location, and we (the developer) do not receive or store any device identifiers or usage data.

Information stored only on your device

A small amount of data is kept in the app's private, sandboxed storage on the device so the app works offline. It is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Favorites — the IDs of videos marked with the heart. They stay on the device and are never transmitted to any external server or third party.
  • The cartoon catalogue — titles and video IDs, cached for up to 24 hours.
  • Rewarded "unlocks" are kept in memory only and are forgotten when the app closes.

Third-party services

To show videos and keep the app free, the app connects to the Google services below. Like any internet service, they receive technical information needed to deliver content — such as the device's IP address, device type and operating system — under their own privacy policies. We do not receive this information.

  • YouTube (Google) — plays the videos through the privacy-enhanced embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com). Thumbnails load from img.youtube.com.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore — downloads the public cartoon catalogue. The app reads from it only; it never writes information about the user.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads, configured for children as described below.

Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob and child-directed treatment · Firebase privacy

Advertising

The app is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Every ad request is configured as follows:

  • Treated as child-directed (tagForChildDirectedTreatment) and under the age of consent, set through the AdMob SDK's age-restricted "child" treatment.
  • Maximum ad content rating: G (general audiences).
  • Non-personalized ads only — no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
  • Ads are clearly labelled in-feed banners, plus optional rewarded videos that play only after a tap. There are no pop-up or app-open ads.
  • Google AdMob takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program.

Parental controls

The grown-ups' area of the app is protected by a parental gate (a multiplication question). Children cannot change any settings or content without passing it.

Children's privacy

The app is directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information has reached us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your choices

You can remove everything the app stores by clearing the app's data in the device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
